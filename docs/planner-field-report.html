Part 3

How one replayed trip works

Take a real past week, say 1–7 Dec 2024. Each night the planner looks 3 days ahead with that night's forecast, does tomorrow, and gets scored on what actually happened. Shots it got count as done, so it moves on to other subjects.

Day it actually doesDays 2–3 it plans around, using a shakier forecast

Every replayed trip is run by five different "players", all scored the same way:

Planner, tomorrow onlyPlans one day at a time with the forecast
Planner, 3 days aheadSaves shots for days 2–3 if they look better
Sun angles onlySame planner, ignores the weather

Part 5

Is "what really happened" right?

The test bench trusts satellites and the Jackson airport. TetonCam in Tetonia, Idaho has saved a picture every 15 minutes since 2014, looking at the west face of the range. Claude is reading one an hour in daylight for both bench winters, so we can see how often the airport's "peaks hidden" call matches what the camera saw.

Result: camera vs airport, hour by hour

1,113 daylight pictures, 8 AM to 4 PM Mountain time, 1 Dec – 31 Jan of both winters · $10.01
Airport: peaks clearAirport: peaks hidden
Camera: peaks clear386 ✓44
Camera: peaks in cloud179482 ✓

They agree 80% of the time. When they disagree, it is almost always the same way: the camera sees cloud on the summits and the airport says clear (179 hours). The airport misses about 1 in 4 hidden-peak hours. These are mostly caps sitting on the high peaks above what the airport reports. Satellite sun agreed with the camera 84% of the time, and it also leans too sunny.

What it means for the results: the test bench is a bit too generous to every player, so the real good-shot counts are lower than the charts show. Everyone is scored by the same truth, so the order is unlikely to change.

The camera is about 30 km from the Grand, on the Idaho side. It's a fair judge of whether the peaks are in cloud; sun on your side of the valley can differ. If the airport and the camera often disagree, I'll re-score the Teton results with the camera.
