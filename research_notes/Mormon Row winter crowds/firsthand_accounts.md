## Q1. How many photographers come to T.A. Moulton Barn at sunrise in winter, compared with autumn? Any Christmas to New Year accounts?

### Takeaway
No source gives a head count for a winter sunrise, and none covers Christmas to New Year week. The numbers that do exist ("5 to 20" photographers depending on season, "probably 20 people" with more arriving) are not tied to winter, and they describe the open-road seasons when people can drive to the barns. The winter sources all point the same way: few people, because in winter you have to walk, ski or snowshoe in, and one local club says you will "likely be the only person around".

### Cited Findings
- A photographer's guide puts the crowd at "between 5 to 20 other photographers at sunrise at the T.A. Moulton Barn, depending on the season". The snippet does not say which season gives which number. — [Photographer's Trail Notes: T.A. Moulton Barn](https://photographerstrailnotes.com/jackson-hole-yellowstone/ta-moulton-barn)
- A TripAdvisor review (season not shown in the snippet) says "there were probably 20 people when they arrived, with more coming closer to sunrise". — [TripAdvisor: "Sunrise at the barn!"](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g60838-d2329299-r230612026-Mormon_Row_Historic_District-Moose_Grand_Teton_National_Park_Wyoming.html)
- An October 2025 TripAdvisor visitor said there were "so many people there that it wasn't worth it" (autumn, for comparison). — [TripAdvisor: Mormon Row Historic District reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60838-d2329299-Reviews-Mormon_Row_Historic_District-Moose_Grand_Teton_National_Park_Wyoming.html)
- A photography guide (no season given): "you'll always find a crowd of photographers gathered here just before sunrise". — [National Park Photographer: T.A. Moulton Barn](https://nationalparkphotographer.com/t-a-moulton-barn/)
- A guide for the open-road season warns that at first light "the parking lot and the road is packed with many cars", and tells you to get there well before sunrise to claim a spot. — [The Mountains Please: Mormon Row sunrise](https://themountainsplease.com/mormon-row-sunrise-photography/)
- The NPS rates the site "very busy" at midday and at sunrise. The rating gives no season, and it sits on the ceremony page, which assumes the open-road season. — [NPS: Mormon Row ceremony location](https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/morowcl.htm)
- Winter, local photography club: "By early winter, most of the visitors are back in their cozy homes, but the barns are left to endure the brutal winter winds, ice and snow." The search summary also reports the article saying that if you hike out in winter you will likely be the only person around. That second part could not be read on the page itself. — [Teton Photography Club: Mormon Row in Winter](https://tetonphotographyclub.org/mormon-row-in-winter/)
- Winter, December 2007: a photographer's gallery titled "Moulton Barn Sunrise 2007" describes a December sunrise reached by a hike in "-26 degrees through modest snow". No other people are mentioned in the snippet. — [Ed Fuhr: Moulton Barn Sunrise 2007](https://www.edfuhr.com/gallery/moultonbarn/)

### Inferences
- The "5 to 20" and "about 20 and growing" numbers almost certainly describe seasons when you can drive to the barns. For 29 Dec to 4 Jan the walk in (about 3/4 to 1 mile each way, see Q2) should cut the sunrise crowd sharply. A reasonable guess is 0 to a handful of photographers on a normal morning. Treat that as an estimate; no source states it.
- Christmas to New Year is the busiest stretch of Jackson's winter, so that week is more likely than mid-January to bring a few people at sunrise, especially after a fresh snow or on a clear morning with an alpenglow forecast. No source confirms this.
- Project context (from this repo's notes, not from the web): the planner's November 2026 plan checks flagged building work at both Mormon Row barns. Work crews or fencing in late December would change both the crowd and the frame. Re-check the NPS conditions page before the trip.

- No account specifically from Christmas to New Year week was found.

## Q2. How busy is it during the day in winter? Do people snowshoe or ski in?

### Takeaway
Yes. In winter the only way to the barns is on foot, skis or snowshoes. Antelope Flats Road is closed to cars from the first snow until spring and is plowed only to a turnout about 3/4 to 1 mile before the Mormon Row junction. Winter accounts describe the walk in and a quiet, "serene" site. None gives numbers for daytime visitors.

### Cited Findings
- NPS: the road to Mormon Row is closed to vehicles from the first snow until late spring, with the closure starting about a mile before the Mormon Row intersection. — [NPS: Mormon Row ceremony location](https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/morowcl.htm)
- NPS "things to do" page: the season is spring, summer and fall, and Antelope Flats Road is closed in winter (page last updated May 2021). — [NPS: Mormon Row](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/mormonrow.htm)
- A local guide: the road is plowed only for the first mile, to a parking turnout, and from there visitors can cross-country ski or snowshoe to the Moulton barn. — [JacksonHoleWY.net: Mormon Row](https://www.jacksonholewy.net/park_highlights/mormon_row.php)
- Teton Photography Club: "only 3/4 mile to the Mormon Row intersection from the ample parking area" in winter. — [Teton Photography Club: Mormon Row in Winter](https://tetonphotographyclub.org/mormon-row-in-winter/)
- TripAdvisor forum thread "Hike to Mormon Row in Winter": one poster puts it at about a mile past the plow turnaround, depending on snow depth. The thread also mentions Gros Ventre Road being plowed only to the forest service gate east of Kelly, a second, longer way in from the south. Post dates could not be read. — [TripAdvisor forum: Hike to Mormon Row in Winter](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g143029-i1019-k14973887-Hike_to_Mormon_Row_in_Winter-Grand_Teton_National_Park_Wyoming.html)
- Winter, January 2014: a TripAdvisor reviewer walked around "in very deep snow" to explore the barns and went three times, at 5 am for sunrise "glowing in the barn window" and again at 7:30 for sunset. The snippet mentions no other people. — [TripAdvisor: "The famous Moulton Barn in Winter"](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g60838-d2329299-r191954415-Mormon_Row_Historic_District-Moose_Grand_Teton_National_Park_Wyoming.html)
- Winter, January 2026: a TripAdvisor review says it was "pretty freezing in January but it was a beautiful place to be". Crowd size is not mentioned in the snippet. — [TripAdvisor: Mormon Row reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60838-d2329299-Reviews-Mormon_Row_Historic_District-Moose_Grand_Teton_National_Park_Wyoming.html)
- A photographer's winter print caption: "It was a long hike on the snowshoes out to the barn." — [Joseph Filer: Mormon Row winter print](https://www.josephfiler.com/photo/wyoming-winter-grand-tetons-barns-0128/)
- Another source: the road is closed in winter, "but the trek in on snowshoes or skis is worth the effort". — [Location Scout: Mormon Settlement Barn](https://www.locationscout.net/usa/245-mormon-settlement-barn)
- A Grand Teton photographer's blog: the area is "even more serene this time of year" in winter, and thick fog is more likely to hide the Tetons. — [Lisa Blount Photography: T.A. Moulton Barn](https://www.lisablountphotography.com/blogs/travel/mormon-row-ta-moulton-barn-travel)
- Tour companies sell winter wildlife tours by vehicle that include Mormon Row. They stop at the road closure; none are ski or sunrise workshops. — [Adventure Collective: private winter wildlife tour](https://app.advguides.com/marketplace/grand-teton-private-winter-wildlife-tour-snake-river-elk-flats-mormon-row)

### Inferences
- In winter, daytime visitors are mostly people walking the plowed-road stub, along with some skiers and snowshoers. The walk in should keep numbers low compared with summer and autumn. No source counts them.
- Distances differ a little (3/4 mile versus about 1 mile to the junction). The John Moulton cluster sits north of the junction and T.A. Moulton south of it, so the walk to each barn is slightly longer than the walk to the junction.

### Gaps
- No winter daytime counts (people per hour, cars at the turnout) were found.
- How full the winter turnout gets at sunrise during the holiday week was not found.

## Q4. Where do people stand (the classic south-east spot at T.A. Moulton, the John Moulton north cluster, the pink house)?

### Takeaway
The search summaries confirm that both barns are photographed at sunrise and that the John Moulton barn has "plenty of room to set up tripods". They give no reliable detail on where people stand in winter. One guide notes that the south barn is easy to reach "except in the winter".

### Cited Findings
- John Moulton Barn: "very popular, particularly at and shortly after sunrise, but there is plenty of room to set up tripods; still, be considerate of other visitors." (season not given) — [PhotoHound: John Moulton Barn](https://photohound.co/spot/john-moulton-barn-1003916)
- The south barn (T.A. Moulton) is "easy to access (except in the winter)". — [Photographer's Trail Notes (search summary)](https://photographerstrailnotes.com/jackson-hole-yellowstone/ta-moulton-barn); a separate page covers the north barns: [Mormon Barn North](https://trailnotes.squarespace.com/jackson-hole-yellowstone/mormon-barn-north)
- A guide to sunrise and sunset spots across Mormon Row: [Where Are Those Morgans: Mormon Row sunrise + sunset guide](https://wherearethosemorgans.com/mormon-row-grand-teton-sunrise-sunset/)

### Inferences
- In winter, with the road closed, people coming from the north turnout reach the John Moulton cluster (and the pink house beside it) first. Getting to T.A. Moulton means walking a little further south along the road, so the T.A. Moulton south-east spot may be the less busy of the two in winter. This is reasoning only; no source says so.

### Gaps
- No winter account says which spot people stood at or how many stood at each.
- Details on the pink house came up in no result.
