# Mormon Row (T.A. Moulton and John Moulton barns): winter access and visitation, late December (29 Dec - 4 Jan)

Research done 27 Nov 2026. Hosts that this session's network refused (could not read the full pages, only search-engine summaries of them): irma.nps.gov (NPS visitor-use statistics, the primary monthly-visits source), home.nps.gov, buckrail.com, jhnewsandguide.com, publiclandsdata.com, nationalsitesguide.com, lisablountphotography.com. www.nps.gov was readable. ## Winter road status and how people physically reach the barns

### Takeaway
In winter the Antelope Flats Road is closed to cars about 1 mile west of the Mormon Row intersection; people park at that pullout (reached from US-191) and walk, snowshoe or ski the unplowed road about a mile to the intersection, then on to each barn. Mormon Row Road itself is not plowed. No NPS wildlife closure covers Mormon Row or Antelope Flats; the nearest one (Kelly Hill / Snake River) is 15 Dec - 31 Mar. Mormon Row also has a construction project (parking lot, restrooms, fencing) paused for winter but not finished.

### Cited Findings
- NPS (ceremony-site page): "Once snow arrives in early fall and until it melts in the late spring," the Mormon Row Historic District is closed to vehicles about 1 mile before the Mormon Row intersection; both the South and North ceremony sites are "Inaccessible by vehicle in the winter." The page names a "parking pullout about 1 mile west on Antelope Flats Road." — [NPS Mormon Row Ceremony Location](https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/morowcl.htm)
- Same page, summer parking capacity (for scale; these lots are behind the winter closure): South site ~12 spaces, North site ~14; "Parking can be very limited especially at sunrise, sunset and midday"; no shoulder parking; the North site is reached by "A short walk down the gated road past the 'Pink House'." — [NPS Mormon Row Ceremony Location](https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/morowcl.htm)
- NPS road page: Antelope Flats, Deadmans Bar, Grassy Lake, Pilgrim Creek, Schwabacher and Two Ocean roads "are closed seasonally based on conditions" (no dates given). US 89/191 and US 26/287 "are plowed and open for winter travel from the town of Jackson to Flagg Ranch." Teton Park Road is closed to cars 1 Nov - 30 Apr (Taggart Lake TH to Signal Mountain Lodge); Moose-Wilson Road closed 1 Nov to mid-May. — [NPS Road Information](https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/roads.htm)
- NPS winter page lists "Antelope Flats Road" among places for cross-country skiing and snowshoeing (no access details); visitor centers close in winter; "Pets are restricted to plowed roadways and parking areas." Page last updated 12 Feb 2026. — [NPS Winter in Grand Teton](https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/winter.htm)
- Third-party guides (search summaries, pages not readable here): the first mile of Antelope Flats Road is plowed to a turnout where skiers/snowshoers start; walk to the John Moulton Barn about 0.9 mi from the parking area; another blog says ~3/4 mile from the Antelope Flats / Mormon Row intersection; a March account found Antelope Flats Road fairly clear but Mormon Row Road unplowed; one blog says the road is plowed in March for bison. — [Travel Experience Live](https://www.travel-experience-live.com/grand-teton-national-park-wyoming/mormon-row/); [Lisa Blount Photography](https://www.lisablountphotography.com/blogs/travel/mormon-row-ta-moulton-barn-travel); [Free Roaming Hiker](https://freeroaminghiker.com/best-winter-hikes-grand-teton-national-park/) (single-source details, unverified)
- Older press: Antelope Flats is among roads closed seasonally (date of article unclear). — [Jackson Hole Radio](https://jacksonholeradio.com/teton-park-roads-closing-for-winter)
- NPS wildlife closures page (updated 9 Dec 2025): "areas around the Snake River, Buffalo Fork River, and Kelly Hill are closed to all public entry" 15 Dec - 31 Mar; summits of Mount Hunt, Prospectors Mountain, Static Peak closed 1 Dec - 30 Apr; bighorn winter zones voluntary 1 Dec - 30 Apr. Mormon Row, Antelope Flats, Blacktail Butte, Uhl Hill and Gros Ventre are not listed. — [NPS Temporary & Wildlife Closures](https://www.nps.gov/grte/learn/management/wildlife-closures.htm)
- A 2013 NPS release listed Snake River floodplain Moran to Menor's Ferry, and Kelly Hill and Uhl Hill, as December - early April wildlife closures (older; Uhl Hill not on the current page). — [NPS 2013 winter release](https://www.nps.gov/grte/learn/news/news-release-13-93.htm)
- Construction: NPS park-wide construction page (updated 4 Nov 2026, per search summary): "Mormon Row Historic District - Open, with some access to buildings closed. Construction ongoing," with sidewalks, picnic tables and fencing being installed; project scope = paved parking lot, restrooms, shade structures. NPS timeframe "June 2026 - 2027"; the park foundation says work through November 2026. — [NPS Park-Wide Construction](https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/park-construction.htm); [GTNP Foundation](https://www.gtnpf.org/blog-post/mormon-row-summer2026); [Cowboy State Daily, 6 May 2026](https://cowboystatedaily.com/2026/05/06/toilets-parking-and-roads-grand-teton-starts-multi-year-improvement-projects/)
- NPS conditions page (updated 23 Nov 2026): "Construction projects will be paused for the winter season from November 15, 2026 to May 1, 2027," though some areas "will remain closed due to site conditions and safety concerns." — [NPS Current Conditions](https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/conditions.htm)
- 2024 winter roads opened to winter recreation on 20 Dec 2024 (Teton Park Road groomed Taggart Lake to Signal Mountain through mid-March); wheeled vehicles incl. fat bikes are not allowed on winter recreation roads. — [NPS news release, Dec 2024](https://www.nps.gov/grte/learn/news/winter-recreation-begins-on-park-roads-in-grand-teton.htm)

### Inferences
- Winter route: US-191 north from Moose Junction to the Antelope Flats turn, drive the plowed first stretch to the winter pullout, then about 1 mile on foot to the intersection; the southern barn cluster is near the intersection and the T.A. Moulton barn a further walk north (guides: ~0.75-0.9 mi to a barn). Round trips of roughly 2-3 miles on snow, so snowshoes or skis plus headlamp for a sunrise shoot. Which barn is how far was not confirmed by an official source.
- Because the car lots sit behind the closure, the sunrise "parking can be very limited" problem of summer does not apply; the limit in winter is the pullout's size and people's willingness to walk a mile in the cold.
- With construction paused 15 Nov 2026 - 1 May 2027, expect fencing / site works left standing and possibly some building access closed during the trip week; check the construction page in late December.

### Gaps
- No official NPS page gives the exact winter plowing end point, the winter pullout's size, or whether the road is gated vs simply unplowed; the "plowed for bison in March" claim is single-source.
- No source on whether the Gros Ventre Road / Kelly side gives winter access to the south end of Mormon Row (Gros Ventre Road status in winter not found on NPS pages read).
- Official walking distances from the winter pullout to each barn not found.

## Grand Teton visitation in December vs other months, and holiday-week counts

### Takeaway
December is one of the park's quietest months: roughly 49,000-50,000 recreation visits in recent years (secondary sites built on NPS data), about 7% of July's ~732,000. No holiday-week (Christmas - New Year) traffic counts or official statements were found.

### Cited Findings
- December averages about 49,334 recreation visits (2021-2025 NPS Visitor Use Statistics), about 7% of the July peak of ~732,000 (search summary of a secondary site; page not readable here). — [NationalSitesGuide](https://www.nationalsitesguide.com/sites/grand-teton/best-time-to-visit/)
- Another secondary site: December average 50,102 (2019-2024), February 54,199 (higher than December). — [TravelBinger](https://travelbinger.com/ideal-times-visit-national-parks)
- Official November counts (for comparison, NPS release 16 Dec 2021): Nov 2016 50,508; Nov 2017 45,496; Nov 2018 44,651; Nov 2019 48,132; Nov 2020 57,039; Nov 2021 61,674 (second-busiest November on record). — [NPS release, 16 Dec 2021](https://www.nps.gov/grte/learn/news/grand-teton-sees-second-busiest-november-for-visitation.htm)
- NPS 2016 release: December 2016 visits were down 18% on December 2015, "likely explained by extreme cold temperatures which deterred visitors," and some traffic counters "malfunctioned on a few days due to the frigid temperatures." Annual totals: 2012 3,918,418; 2013 4,117,323; 2014 4,296,512; 2015 4,647,886; 2016 4,822,972 (pre-2018 counting method). — [NPS 2016 Visitation Sets Record](https://www.nps.gov/grte/learn/news/2016-visitation-sets-record.htm)
- Annual totals under the current method: 2018 3,491,151 (record at the time); 2021 over 3.8 million; 2023 3,417,106 (+22% on 2022); 2024 3,628,222; 2025 about 3.80 million (aggregator). — [NPS Nov 2021 release](https://www.nps.gov/grte/learn/news/grand-teton-sees-second-busiest-november-for-visitation.htm); [NPS 2023 release](https://www.nps.gov/grte/learn/news/325-5-million-visits-to-national-parks-in-2023-3-4-million-visits-at-grand-teton.htm); [Buckrail, 2024 totals](https://buckrail.com/yellowstone-grand-teton-nps-report-record-visitation-in-2024/); [WhenToVisitParks](https://www.whentovisitparks.com/park/grand-teton-national-park)
- NPS (Dec 2021): "Limited services and seasonal road and wildlife closures make a winter visit far different than a summer experience." — [NPS release, 16 Dec 2021](https://www.nps.gov/grte/learn/news/grand-teton-sees-second-busiest-november-for-visitation.htm)

### Inferences
- ~50,000 December visits is about 1,600 a day on average for the whole park, and the count includes people driving US-191 through the park (recreation visits are counted at traffic counters). Only a small share would walk a mile in to Mormon Row; the holiday week is likely above the December daily average because that is Jackson's peak lodging week (next section).
- Cold snaps cut December visits (2016: -18%), so a very cold holiday week would mean fewer people at the barns.

### Gaps
- Exact December totals per year 2018-2025 from NPS IRMA (irma.nps.gov blocked in this session).
- No daily or holiday-week traffic counter data or NPS statement about Christmas - New Year traffic found.

## Jackson Hole holiday-week tourism (crowd proxy)

### Takeaway
The week between Christmas and New Year's is traditionally one of Jackson Hole's busiest winter weeks, with New Year's weekend around three-quarters full in past seasons; occupancy then drops in the first week of January.

### Cited Findings
- JH News&Guide (search summary; page blocked here): the chamber's 31 Oct forecast was 50% occupancy for the week between Christmas and New Year's, "traditionally one of the busiest times of the season," before bookings picked up with the snow (article URL suggests Dec 2024, year not confirmed). — [JH News&Guide](https://www.jhnewsandguide.com/news/business/lodging-bookings-begin-to-rise-as-snow-flies/article_1f90e2d4-bf56-11ef-b8cd-a3f6ccf4b0e8.html)
- Buckrail (winter 2020-21, search summary): New Year's weekend occupancy 76.1%; the week of 18 Dec rose from 23% to 50% average; Christmas week on par with FY19/20, "but the first week of January we see a drop off in occupancy." — [Buckrail](https://buckrail.com/jackson-hole-tourism-reports-for-dec-projections-for-winter-2021/)
- Buckrail: "Winter tourism off to a slow start" (year not confirmed from summary). — [Buckrail](https://buckrail.com/winter-tourism-off-to-slower-start/)
- Visit Jackson Hole: winter lodging averages ~$500-600 a night at its highest; winter is "not as busy as summer (50% to 75% total occupancy)." — [Visit Jackson Hole](https://visitjacksonhole.com/off-season-vs-peak-season-in-jackson-hole)
- zRankings (2026-27 guide): crowds at the ski resort are small until the week after Christmas. — [zRankings](https://www.zrankings.com/jackson-hole-december)

### Inferences
- 29-31 Dec is likely the peak crowd window of the trip; 2-4 Jan should be quieter. Most holiday visitors come for skiing at Jackson Hole Mountain Resort, not the park, so the barns should still be far less busy than in summer or autumn.

### Gaps
- No resort skier-visit numbers for the holiday week found; no 2025-26 or 2026-27 holiday occupancy forecast found.
- No Teton County / Travel & Tourism Board lodging report read directly (sites blocked or not found).

## Official NPS statements on Mormon Row visitation in winter

### Takeaway
None found. The only official winter facts are that the district is closed to vehicles in the snow season (about 1 mile before the intersection) and that summer parking is tight at sunrise and sunset.

### Cited Findings
- "Parking can be very limited especially at sunrise, sunset and midday" (ceremony page, applies to the season when the lots are reachable). — [NPS Mormon Row Ceremony Location](https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/morowcl.htm)
- A photography blog warns the area gets busy around sunrise and in peak seasons (not winter-specific). — [Lisa Blount Photography](https://www.lisablountphotography.com/blogs/travel/mormon-row-ta-moulton-barn-travel)

### Inferences
- In late December the mile-long walk is the main crowd filter; expect a handful of photographers at sunrise on clear holiday mornings rather than the summer tripod lines (no source counts this).

### Gaps
- No NPS count, statement or study of Mormon Row winter visitor numbers found; the 2022 summer visitor-use study covers summer only ([NPS Summer 2022 Key Insights](https://www.nps.gov/grte/learn/upload/Summer-2022-Key-Insights-and-Findings_508.pdf), not read).
